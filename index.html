Website Released!

July 23, 2026

Welcome to the official Stud Studios website! We're excited to finally open the doors to a brand new home for our community. Whether you're here because you enjoy LEGO builds, Minecraft adventures, tutorials, or just want to stay up to date with everything happening at Stud Studios, you've come to the right place.

This website is designed to be the central hub for everything Stud Studios. Here you'll find the latest news, official merchandise, Minecraft server information, tutorials, community updates, and much more as the website continues to grow. Our goal is to make it easier than ever for viewers and community members to find everything they're looking for in one place.

The website was designed and developed by Felix Haney, who worked closely with Stud Studios owner Cy Miller to create a clean, modern experience that reflects the channel's focus on creativity and building. Together, they wanted a website that combines the worlds of LEGO and Minecraft while providing useful resources for the entire community.

This is only the beginning. More pages, tutorials, merchandise, community events, and new features are already planned for future updates. Thank you for visiting, and we hope you'll continue to join us as Stud Studios continues to grow.

Work Begins On MC Server

May 1, 2026

The official Stud Studios Minecraft Server is now open for everyone to enjoy! Whether you're a longtime fan of the channel or a brand-new visitor, the server is designed to give players a fun and welcoming place to build, explore, and make new friends.

Every new player begins their adventure in the Main City. The city acts as the heart of the server, where you'll find important buildings, shops, helpful information, and other players. If you're just getting started, you can spend your first nights at the city's tavern before heading out into the wilderness to begin your own journey.

Once you're ready, you can leave the safety of the city and carve out your own path. Build a home, start a village with friends, or travel far from civilization in search of valuable resources. Trading plays an important role on the server, and players are encouraged to create shops, exchange supplies, and build a thriving economy together.

Whether you choose to stay close to the city or venture thousands of blocks away, every player's story becomes part of the Stud Studios world. We can't wait to see the incredible builds, businesses, and communities that everyone creates together.

DEVS: GoofyMooCow, Spy Cow, StudStudios, Wasabi_Warrior21.
Thank you to all who helped build this server. We would also like to thank the players who helped build it up!

📖 Server Rules

Help keep the Stud Studios SMP fun and welcoming.

1. Respect Others

Treat other players with respect. Harassment and unnecessary arguments aren't welcome.

2. No Griefing

Don't destroy, steal, or intentionally damage another player's builds or belongings.

3. No Cheating

Don't use cheats, hacks, or exploits to gain an unfair advantage.

4. Build Responsibly

Be considerate of other players when choosing where and how you build.

5. PVP Rules

If you are going to PVP another player, both players must agree. You then make specific rules, example: Winner gets losers equiptment.

6. Protect Your Builds

Use signs or fences to mark your base. Don't build too close to someone else. THE WORLD IS INFINITE.

7. No Lagging

Avoid massive redstone clocks, mob farms with thousands of entities, or anything that tanks server performance.

8. Respect Staff

If staff says to stop something, stop. Report issues instead of trying to handle them yourself.

9. Have Fun!

Most importantly, enjoy the server, make awesome creations, and have fun!
